The problem

High-stakes problems
don’t stay in one lane.

A regulatory change becomes a public story. A cyber incident becomes a compliance question and a contract dispute. A data center project needs a permit, a community and a power deal at the same time.

Most organizations manage these with separate advisors who rarely see each other’s work. Each one solves their piece. Nobody owns the whole problem, and the gaps between advisors are where the risk lives.

We built Gray Reed Advisory so the whole problem has one team.

When to call

When clients call us

Our work usually starts where policy, reputation, risk and operations collide.

The best time to call is before the issue has a name. The second-best time is now.

  1. A bill, rule or permit review threatens your business model
  2. A reputational issue is about to become a headline
  3. A deal closes and IT, cyber and compliance gaps surface
  4. A regulator, auditor, insurer or customer asks for proof of your controls
  5. A major purchase, RFP or vendor contract needs a defensible process
  6. A data center or infrastructure project needs legal, legislative and community support at once
Who we are

An advisory firm
with a law firm behind it.

Gray Reed Advisory Services is the business advisory affiliate of Gray Reed, a full-service Texas law firm.

We focus on four services: Government Affairs and Public Relations, delivered by our public affairs division GRPR, plus Risk & Compliance and Procurement. Two shared solutions, Crisis Command and GRIDS, bring our advisors and Gray Reed’s attorneys together on the most complex work.

We serve companies, trade associations, public entities and project developers across Texas.
